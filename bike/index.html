model & assumptions

everything is a steady-state power balance: at constant speed, what the rider puts into the cranks (minus drivetrain loss) equals the power burned against gravity, rolling resistance, and air.

η·P = v · [ m·g·(sinθ + Crr·cosθ) + ½·ρ·CdA·(v + w)·|v + w| ]

in "power → speed" mode the page solves this for v (largest root, so tailwinds behave). in "speed → power" mode it's just evaluated. cadence then falls out of speed and gear: cadence = v / (wheel circumference × chainring/cog), and torque is P / ω.

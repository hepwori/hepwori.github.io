The Price of Sunshine

What pleasant weather adds to the cost of a home. Each dot is one of 54 major U.S. metros, plotted by average hours of sunshine per year against median home value, and colored by region.

City Region Sunshine hours / yr Median home price

Sunshine: average annual hours of sunshine, NOAA-derived, mostly via Current Results; 6 cities missing there (Fresno, Honolulu, Los Angeles, San Francisco, Tucson, Tulsa) filled from Wikipedia's sunshine-duration table (same underlying climate-normal data, summed from monthly figures — cross-checked to the hour against independently reported totals for the same cities). Two cities (marked *, dashed dot outline) have no published sunshine-hours figure anywhere found and are estimated instead: Grand Rapids, from NOAA's percent-of-possible-sunshine for that city scaled by a possible-sunshine-hours constant calibrated against nearby Great Lakes stations; and Boulder, using nearby Denver's figure as a proxy (Boulder has no independent climate-normal station of its own). See the README for the full method. Home prices: Zillow Home Value Index (all homes, smoothed & seasonally adjusted), July 2026, via Zillow Research. Regions are hand-assigned Census-style groupings, adapted for this chart — a supplementary layer; city identity always rides on the label, hover, and table, never on color alone.
